# TryHackMe: SOC Roles in Blue Team

## Room Overview

This room introduced the different security departments within an organization and explained how they work together to protect a company's systems, networks, and information.

## What I Learned

### 1. Role of the CISO

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) oversees an organization's cybersecurity strategy. While executives like the CEO focus on business objectives, the CISO focuses on the company's security needs and helps establish the appropriate security departments.

### 2. Security Departments

Security departments can vary depending on the size and needs of an organization.

- **Red Team:** Offensive security professionals who identify weaknesses through penetration testing and ethical hacking.
- **GRC Team:** Focuses on governance, risk, compliance, security policies, and regulations such as PCI DSS.
- **Blue Team:** Defensive security professionals who protect company systems and respond to potential security threats. Examples include SOC analysts, security engineers, and incident responders.

## Key Takeaways

One thing that stood out to me was how the responsibilities of cybersecurity roles can vary depending on the organization.

Not every company has the same security departments. The responsibilities and authority of each role can differ based on the company's size, structure, and security needs.
